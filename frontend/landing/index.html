Built on MCP

Your finances, native to your AI.

The Model Context Protocol is the open standard that lets AI agents call real tools. WealthAgent is an MCP server — so your money becomes something your agent can query directly, no plugins or copy-paste.

1

One endpoint, any client

Add a single MCP URL to Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor, or any MCP-compatible app. It just works.

2

Real tools, real data

Your agent calls typed tools like wealth_accounts_list, wealth_tx_list, and wealth_gains — answers come from live Plaid data, not guesses.

3

Scoped & revocable keys

Mint a token per agent, read-only by default. See when each was last used and revoke any of them in one click.

claude_desktop_config.json
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "wealth": {
      "url": "https://app.texasnetworth.com/mcp",
      "headers": {
        "Authorization": "Bearer wa_pat_•••"
      }
    }
  }
}

How it works

Connected in three steps.

No data exports, no spreadsheets, no copy-pasting balances into a chat window. Link once, hand your agent a key, and ask away.

STEP 01

Link your accounts

Connect banks, brokerages, credit cards, and loans through Plaid. Everything aggregates into one continuously-synced picture of your finances.

STEP 02

Mint a key for your agent

Generate a scoped access token in the Connect your AI tab and drop it into your agent's MCP config. One paste — done.

STEP 03

Just ask

"What did I spend on travel last month?" "What are my realized gains this year?" Your AI answers from live, real data — not guesses.

What your agent can do

One key. The whole financial toolbox.

Every capability is exposed as an MCP tool your agent can call directly — read-only by default.

Net worth & portfolio

Assets, liabilities, holdings breakdown, and performance over any range — at a glance or on demand.

Transaction search

Filter, search, tag, and annotate every transaction across accounts. Your agent does the digging.

Capital gains & tax

FIFO realized gains, cost-basis overrides, and deductible tagging — tax season without the shoebox.

Spending insights

Ask where the money went by category, merchant, or month. Real numbers, instant answers.

Live sync on demand

Tell your agent to pull the latest from your institutions before it answers — always current.

Rename & organize

Give accounts human nicknames; your agent uses them so its answers read like you talk.

Built for trust

Your data stays yours.

The whole point is access without surrender. You decide which agents connect, what they can touch, and you cut them off in one click.

Read-only by default

Agents see your data; they can't move money or change your accounts.

Keys you control

Mint a token per agent, see when each was last used, and revoke any of them instantly.

Encrypted & never sold

Credentials are handled by Plaid and stored encrypted. Your financial data never trains a model and is never sold.
