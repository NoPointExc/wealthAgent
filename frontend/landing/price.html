Questions

Is the free self-hosted version really the full product?

Yes. WealthAgent is open source — the hosted plans are the exact same software, run and maintained by us so you don't have to manage a server, database, backups, or your own Plaid credentials.

What do I need to self-host?

A machine that runs Docker, and your own Plaid API keys. Everything else ships in the repo. Your data stays entirely on your own server. See the GitHub project for setup.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, from the billing portal. You keep full access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Do you sell my data or train models on it?

Never. Your financial data is never sold and is never used to train AI models — on any plan.
