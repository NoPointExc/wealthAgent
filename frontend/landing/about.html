About

Your money, native to your AI.

WealthAgent is a product of Texas Net Worth LLC. We build the missing bridge between your financial life and the AI agents you already use.

Why we built it

AI assistants have become genuinely useful — but they're flying blind about the one thing that touches every decision you make: your money. Copy-pasting balances into a chat window is tedious, error-prone, and stale the moment you hit send.

WealthAgent fixes that. We aggregate every bank, brokerage, credit card, and loan through Plaid and expose it over the Model Context Protocol (MCP) — the open standard that lets AI agents call real tools. Point Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or any MCP client at one secure endpoint and ask about your net worth, holdings, spending, or capital gains. It reads live data, on demand, and it's read-only by default.

What we believe

Access without surrender

You decide which agents connect, what they can touch, and you cut them off in one click. Your financial data is never sold and never used to train AI models.

Open by default

WealthAgent is open source. Run it yourself for free, or let us host it for you. No lock-in either way.
